# ENTLORE: A Graph-Grounded Benchmark for Latent Organizational Reasoning in Enterprise Question Answering

## Relevance decision

This refresh-window preprint is relevant because it provides primary academic evidence about organization-memory, enterprise QA, decision systems, benchmark for long-horizon organizational or agent memory systems. It is theory/tooling evidence rather than an organization case study.

## Preserved abstract evidence

Enterprise question answering is framed as retrieving internal documents and generating grounded answers. Routine enterprise records, however, are work by-products in which required organizational relations remain implicit across heterogeneous sources. ENTLORE reconstructs an audited enterprise world from routine documents, authoritative organizational tables, and operational records; versioned organizational conventions certify derived relations in a truth graph, enabling complete golden answers and proof certificates.
